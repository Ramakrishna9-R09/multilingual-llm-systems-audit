Part A · tokenizer audit

Hold meaning constant, not word count.

A parallel sentence is a better cost denominator than a whitespace word, grapheme, or byte: it approximately fixes the message being expressed.

01

Audit the script, not just its output

split(" ") creates empty words when spaces repeat. Lowercasing also mutates real inputs. Both effects were isolated with before/after measurements.

02

Keep the harmless normalization

NFC normalizes canonically equivalent Unicode strings. In the controlled café probe, raw NFD costs 4 GPT-2 tokens while NFC costs 3.

03

Do not swap a tokenizer alone

The XLM-R result motivates evaluating a compatible multilingual model family. Tokenizer vocabulary, embeddings, and model weights are coupled.

Part B · capacity reconciliation

Reported throughput is not always useful throughput.

Long prompts inflated a prompt-plus-output rate. The audit separates it from generated-token goodput and connects the drop at batch 32 to KV pressure.

Where the scheduler breaks

At batch 24, KV utilization is 0.93 with no preemption. At 32 it is 0.97, seven sequences are preempted, and output goodput falls 13.9%.

Part C · launch choice

Start reversible.

Path

Feature-flagged prompt-only style control. No SFT or second model call before validation.

Success gate

At least 65% blinded preference and ≤2% critical failures, separately for Hindi and Kannada.

Kill criterion

By week 2, stop if either language has <60% preference, >5% critical failures, or weak reviewer agreement.
