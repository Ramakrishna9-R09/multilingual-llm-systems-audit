Audit the script, not just its output

split(" ") creates empty words when spaces repeat. Lowercasing also mutates real inputs. Both effects were isolated with before/after measurements.

02

Keep the harmless normalization

NFC normalizes canonically equivalent Unicode strings. In the controlled café probe, raw NFD costs 4 GPT-2 tokens while NFC costs 3.

03

Do not swap a tokenizer alone

The XLM-R result motivates evaluating a compatible multilingual model family. Tokenizer vocabulary, embeddings, and model weights are coupled.

Part C · launch choice

Start reversible.

Path

Feature-flagged prompt-only style control. No SFT or second model call before validation.

Success gate

At least 65% blinded preference and ≤2% critical failures, separately for Hindi and Kannada.

Kill criterion

By week 2, stop if either language has <60% preference, >5% critical failures, or weak reviewer agreement.

A planned validation track

Three weeks to a defensible launch decision.

These are planned gates from the decision memo, not claimed product results. The plan validates Hindi and Kannada first, where native-speaker review is available.

Day 1

Fix the evaluation set

Draft 100 situations across Hindi and Kannada. Generate baseline plus four prompt variants before the reviewer sees any labels.

Weeks 1–2

Run blinded comparisons

Use the available 20 reviewer hours for roughly 800 pairwise judgements, including calibration and high-risk samples.

End of week 2

Pass or stop

Require ≥65% preference, Wilson lower bound >55%, ≤2% critical failures, and ≥80% duplicate agreement.

Week 3 · review

Enable only if earned

Keep the prompt-only control behind a feature flag. Kill it if either language is <60% preference, has >5% critical failures, or reviewer agreement is <70%.
